# Thumbnail prompts — 2026.09.25 World Harnesses

16:9, 1376×768, Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro). White bengal cat, dark cinematic lab, teal/cyan and
magenta rim light, haze. Top-left third stays clear for the title card. Title text is bold white
condensed uppercase, top-left, 1–3 words per line.

## Prompt 1 — Self-tightening harness

**Scene:** A dark cinematic robotics lab, haze catching teal and magenta rim light from unseen panels.
Thin cables and monitor glow line the background, out of focus.

**Cat pose/action:** The white bengal cat sits upright wearing a mechanical harness of thin straps and
brushed-metal buckles across its chest and back. Two small robot arms hover beside it, mid-motion,
threading and re-tightening a strap on its own harness — the cat is calm, eyes half-closed, as if the
adjustment is routine self-maintenance rather than something done to it.

**Lighting/palette:** Dark charcoal background, cold cyan key light from camera-left, magenta rim light
from camera-right catching the harness buckles and the cat's fur edge. Thin haze in the light shafts.

**Composition:** Cat and robot arms occupy the right two-thirds of the frame, centered low-right so the
harness detail reads at thumbnail size. Top-left third empty and dark for the title.

**Title text:** "WORLD" / "HARNESSES" — bold white condensed uppercase, stacked two lines, top-left.

*Why this idea:* Literalizes the deck title and its lead theme (harness self-improvement, t1) — the
system tuning its own control layer, not an external hand doing it.

## Prompt 2 — Walls built from a point cloud

**Scene:** A half-formed room: one side is solid dark lab wall and shelving, the other side dissolves
into a sparse wireframe/point-cloud lattice still assembling itself in mid-air, teal points denser near
the cat and thinning toward magenta haze at the edges.

**Cat pose/action:** The white bengal cat walks forward through the boundary between the solid half and
the point-cloud half, one paw stepping into a patch of geometry that is still resolving — points
snapping into a solid tile under its paw as it lands.

**Lighting/palette:** Dark cinematic lab base, cyan glow from the point-cloud lattice itself as the main
light source on the cat's near side, magenta haze bleeding in from the unresolved far side.

**Composition:** Cat mid-stride through the center-right of the frame, walking left to right so its
forward motion reads at a glance; the point-cloud wall fills the right third. Top-left third stays plain
dark lab wall for the title.

**Title text:** "WORLD" / "HARNESSES" — bold white condensed uppercase, stacked two lines, top-left.

*Why this idea:* Captures what t2 shares — camera-controlled, geometry-native world models that build
scene memory as they're explored (WorldCrafter, GAE) — as a single glanceable image of a world forming
under the cat's own steps.

## Prompt 3 — Grounded grasp

**Scene:** A dark robotics lab workbench, teal task-light pooling on the tabletop, magenta rim light
from a gantry behind. A small object (a metal bolt or block) sits on the bench.

**Cat pose/action:** The white bengal cat reaches one paw toward the object; a thin glowing wireframe box
has locked onto the object exactly at paw's width away, with three faint cyan tether lines running from
the box's corners up to a small camera/sensor module mounted above — the cat's paw hasn't touched the
object yet, but the box shows it's already "grounded."

**Lighting/palette:** Dark background falling to black at the edges, warm-cool contrast kept to
teal/cyan (the wireframe box, the tether lines) and magenta (rim light on the cat and bench edge), haze
softening the tether lines.

**Composition:** Bench and object sit center-right at eye level with the cat's reaching paw, wireframe
box small and sharp so it reads even at thumbnail size. Top-left third clear dark lab background for the
title.

**Title text:** "WORLD" / "HARNESSES" — bold white condensed uppercase, stacked two lines, top-left.

*Why this idea:* Stands in for t3 — the shared move of putting explicit structure (3D grounding, action
tokens, distilled features) under a policy instead of relying on end-to-end scale — shown as a box that
locks onto the object before the paw ever gets there.
