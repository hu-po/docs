World models with memory and geometry

Four world models from this week: 3D-aware scene memory, geometry-native latents, one predictive interface across domains, and real-time keyboard-and-text control.

WorldCrafter: camera-controlled exploration of static (a) and dynamic (b) scenes with reconstructed point clouds, subjects re-entering view (c), and scenes generated from text alone (d).

Yu et al. · WorldCrafter · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.24984

WorldCrafter-generated video that explores a classroom and revisits previously observed viewpoints under WASD and arrow-key camera control.

WorldCrafter project page

Zing-0.5: twelve generated environments across visual styles, from photoreal city and desert scenes to pixel-art, kart-racing and anime worlds.

Chen et al. · Zing-0.5 · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.17909

Six frames from one interactive Zing-0.5 session: key symbols show movement inputs, pink labels show text instructions (cheer, open an umbrella), with recording timestamps.

Chen et al. · Zing-0.5 · Fig. 2 · arXiv:2609.17909

GAE: Stage 1 compresses frozen geometry-foundation features into a diffusable latent with a separate RGB head; Stage 2 flow-matches generated-view latents conditioned on reference latents, camera rays and text.

Lu et al. · GAE · Fig. 2 · arXiv:2609.24981

GAE image-to-video generation on RealEstate10K, shown as progressive RGB and geometry output.

GAE project page

JEPA-Anything scenario atlas: domain contexts and outputs across terminal readout, latent world dynamics and scientific analysis, all through one factorized predictive-state interface.

Cui et al. · JEPA-Anything · Fig. 2 · arXiv:2609.20800

(a) Relative MSE reduction of JEPA-Anything vs standard JEPA on nine prediction tasks (five-seed means). (b) CausalWorld closed-loop control return, mean ± SD over five seeds.

Cui et al. · JEPA-Anything · Fig. 5 · arXiv:2609.20800

02 / 03

Grounding robot policies

Four robot papers put explicit structure under the policy: 3D object grounding, a command interface for an off-the-shelf VLM, an action tokenizer, and features distilled from a world model.

Grounded Action Model: language, 2D point or box prompts go through a pretrained 3D grounding model into image and detection tokens for action prediction, evaluated in and out of domain.

Zhang et al. · Grounded Action Model · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.23863

GAM rollout on a Franka arm: sort objects into bowls, out-of-distribution scene.

Grounded Action Model project page

Robo Dawn transfers a general-purpose VLM to robot control through a human-intuitive action interface and in-context demonstrations.

Guo et al. · Robo Dawn · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.22966

Robo Dawn loop: the VLM sees multi-view observations, state, feedback and memory, reasons, and emits semantic commands that are grounded into planned motions; outcomes return as feedback.

Guo et al. · Robo Dawn · Fig. 2 · arXiv:2609.22966

ActionPiece tokenizer: each eight-step action chunk becomes 16 discrete tokens; anchor, near and far chunks by physical action distance train a rank-preservation loss alongside reconstruction.

Lian et al. · ActionPiece · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.18487

Within-cohort success rank vs (a) reconstruction-fidelity rank and (b) physical-rank-consistency rank over 55 evaluations; (c) LIBERO-Plus success of matched tokenizers.

ActionPiece project page

THAW-VLA (0.8B): (a) LIBERO four-suite mean success vs parameter count for VLAs and world-model policies; (b) peak VRAM and latency of one inference against a 32 GB RTX 5090.

Dao et al. · THAW-VLA · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.24682

THAW-VLA (0.8B) real-robot rollouts: single-arm fruit and egg pick-and-place (AgileX Nero), bimanual fruit handover (TRIP-Bag); inference on one RTX 5090.

THAW-VLA project page

Harness self-improvement

The prompts, tools, memory and control flow around a frozen model are searched, regularized and distilled like weights. One week after the 09.18 recursive self-improvement stream.

SoL-Pi: a research AI inspects execution traces, proposes harness changes and filters them through capability and cost gates; (b) held-out EdgeBench score and API cost vs Codex, Claude Code and Pi.

Liu et al. · SoL-Pi · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.20519

SoL-Pi Action Fusion: baseline needs three model API calls for an edit, its follow-up command and the next action; the fused harness runs the follow-up locally after a successful edit, using two.

SoL-Pi project page

RRSI overview: proposal-side constraints control how search capacity is used; selection-side constraints control which measured improvements become permanent harness state.

Xia et al. · RRSI · Fig. 2 · arXiv:2609.24972

(a) Relative gain on the evolve split vs on OOD held-out tasks for harness-evolution methods; dashed line is 1:1 transfer. (b–d) OOD held-out score of initial harness H₀, prior-method average and RRSI.

Xia et al. · RRSI · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.24972

Harness-Zero: (1) evolve a private harness; (2) a harnessing agent passes or replaces each student proposal under the fixed target harness; (3) SFT the student on reviewed trajectories and deploy it under the target harness alone.

Ye et al. · Harness-Zero · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.24974

EvoOntology: a data agent exploring heterogeneous sources directly (a) vs through an ontology layer revised by a diagnose–attribute–patch–evaluate loop (b).

Chong et al. · EvoOntology · Fig. 1 · arXiv:2609.15779

EvoOntology explorer, content layer of one ontology version: terms, mappings, constraints and evidence nodes with semantic and structural edges.
